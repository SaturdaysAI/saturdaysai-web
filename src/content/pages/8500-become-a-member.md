## Find your place in Saturdays.AI

Learn with others, share what you know and build projects with social impact. There are different ways to take part.

- [Find a local community](https://saturdays.ai/cities/) to meet learners and mentors.
- [Explore the courses](https://saturdays.ai/online/) and choose your next learning step.
- [Become a community leader](https://saturdays.ai/create-your-own-ai-saturdays/) or share your experience as a mentor.

[Contact the community team](mailto:info@saturdays.ai) to discuss how you can contribute.
