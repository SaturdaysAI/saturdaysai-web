## Start an AI Saturdays community

If there is no active community in your city, you can help create one. We welcome people who want to organise, support others and share what they know: community leaders, mentors and ambassadors from different backgrounds.

AI Saturdays combines shared Saturday sessions with independent work during the week. We learn AI together and develop projects with social impact in mind, connecting people and organisations within and beyond each city.

[Start with the welcome course](https://cursos.saturdays.ai/courses/saturdaysai-community-onboarding)

![](/media/13aa42fd7194f063.webp)

[Discover how AI Saturdays works](https://saturdays.ai/ai-saturdays/)

### A community that grows with you

We have supported more than 30,000 AI fellows. Our archive also records a network of over 300 ambassadors and a presence in more than 30 cities; explore the current communities to find your starting point.

### Connect with people who share your curiosity

Organising a community helps you meet learners, practitioners, mentors and people who want to build something useful with AI. You can share ideas, find collaborators and learn from different experiences.

### Learn how to organise, too

Alongside technical knowledge, a community needs coordination, communication and care for its members. The team can guide you through the resources and support available as you prepare your initiative.

### From an idea to your first meeting

#### 1. Tell us what motivates you

You do not need to be an expert in every area. We want to understand your motivations and help you connect with other people in your city.

![AI Saturdays ambassadors](/media/8c39f88758380feb.webp)

#### 2. Prepare your community

Get to know our mission, programmes, resources and organising responsibilities. We will agree on the next steps with you and discuss how to support the process.

![](/media/aad3c993c071d715.webp)

![](/media/80b008313b7d2afe.webp)

#### 3. Organise an edition

The programme model proposes a team of two or three people, with at least one person experienced in machine learning. Saturdays.AI provides guidance and learning and organisational resources. Confirm the format, requirements and available support with the team before announcing your edition.
