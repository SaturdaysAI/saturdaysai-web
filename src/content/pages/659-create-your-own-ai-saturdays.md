## Join or Create an  
AI Saturdays community

**AI Saturdays** is a part-time (saturdays sync, rest of the week async), in person bootcamp, where passionate individuals learn AI together and code social impact projects **#ai4all #ai4good**

At the same time, Saturdays.AI connects the local and global ecosystem of experts, individuals and organizations to create social impact through A.I.

Can’t find an active community in your city? Become a leader, mentor or ambassador of Saturdays.AI and create your own!

### Why Saturdays.AI

#### Connect with other people that share your passion

Saturdays.AI is a great place to connect with passionate people driven to build something new with AI tools. There is no better place to meet new friends, colleagues, mentors, cofounders, and investors

#### Improve your knowledge

Sometimes you really need more social and organizational skills and knowledge to go to the next level. Saturdays.AI also goes deep on pactical skill sets. Just what you need to progress quickly.

As a Saturdays.AI organizer you have access to the online master and premium programs to continue learning.

#### Start something great

Knowing you’re not alone can make all the difference. Saturdays.AI provides participants many opportunities to engage and connect with like-minded individuals — and truly start A.I. amazing career or projects.

Access job opportunities, resources and grants for your own AI project.

### Join us

![AI\_Saturdays\_Ambassadors](/media/8c39f88758380feb.webp)

#### Find ambassadors

Saturdays.AI is composed of very diverse people; you don’t need to be an expert. 

We will contact you to get to **know you**, **your motivations** and to see the best way in which we can help and connect with other people like you in your city.

#### Organize a community

During the first few weeks you will learn the **basic clues and points** you need to know as an organiser (benefits, community, resources, etc.), who we are, the programmes we offer, and what it really means to be part of Saturdays.AI. 

Throughout this process you will have a mentor by your side to guide you and help you with everything. 

![](/media/aad3c993c071d715.webp)

![](/media/80b008313b7d2afe.webp)

#### Launch AI Saturdays

AI Saturdays is the main programme we organise, aimed at enabling local communities to learn AI in a practical way while creating social impact projects.

You will need to set up a **2-3 people team** to carry out the initiative. At least one of them should have a background in Machine Learning. From Saturdays.AI, we will provide the rest of the didactic and organisational resources.
