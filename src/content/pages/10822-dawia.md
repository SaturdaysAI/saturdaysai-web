## DAW + AI

DAWIA is an initiative from Saturdays.AI and Escola Joan XXIII that combines web application development, artificial intelligence and cloud technologies. The proposal brings together teachers and AI tools to support learning.

This page describes the programme as originally announced. Contact DAWIA to confirm the current offer, entry requirements and recognition of each qualification.

[Visit the DAWIA website](https://dawia.es/)

![Saturdays.AI training](/media/6be21da428514174.webp)

### Three areas of study

- CFGS DAW: Spain’s higher vocational qualification in Web Application Development.
- Specialisation in artificial intelligence and big data.
- Preparation for AWS cloud developer certification.

The programme was designed to run over two years. Preparing for a certification does not mean automatically receiving it: each qualification or certification has its own assessment requirements.

![AI4Schools educational programme](/media/c478286f57984fc0.webp)

### The proposed learning approach

- Personalised AI support for practice and questions, alongside guidance from teachers.
- Work with software development tools such as React, AWS, Google Cloud, Azure DevOps, Confluence and Jira.
- Teaching from technology practitioners.

The announced format was online and mainly asynchronous, with an emphasis on placements at organisations that work remotely. Ask the team about current placement conditions and availability.

[Find out about the current programme](https://dawia.es/)
