## Social impact projects

Here you will find some AI projects carried out by Fellows from all over the world. If you also want to create a project and learn how to apply artificial intelligence to societal problems, [find your campus](https://saturdays.ai/cities/)  or join our [online Master](https://saturdays.ai/master-ia-online/).

You can try some [online demos](https://huggingface.co/Saturdays). And if you don’t find the project you are looking for, you can take a look at our [Medium page](https://medium.com/saturdays-ai).
